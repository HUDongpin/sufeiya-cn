Reading 2 / 2

Finding event information

待开始

阅读英文材料，判断哪一处变化最能解释结果。只保存第一次选择。

The student newspaper originally sent one long newsletter every month. Readers often missed club announcements because the notices appeared near the end. The editors began sending a shorter weekly bulletin with a separate events section. More students opened the event links, even though the total number of announcements stayed about the same.

Why did more students open the event links?
